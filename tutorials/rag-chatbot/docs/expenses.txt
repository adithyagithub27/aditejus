Travel and expenses (sample company handbook)

Train and bus fares are reimbursed in full with a ticket or receipt.

Flights are allowed for trips over 500 km and must be economy class, booked through the travel desk.

Hotel stays are reimbursed up to Rs 4,000 per night in metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad) and Rs 2,500 per night elsewhere.

Meals on travel days are covered up to Rs 800 per day without receipts.

Taxis and ride apps are reimbursed for business trips; keep the trip receipt.

Expense claims must be submitted in the finance portal within 30 days of the trip.

Claims above Rs 25,000 need the department head's approval.
