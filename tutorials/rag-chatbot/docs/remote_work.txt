Remote work (sample company handbook)

Employees may work from home up to 2 days a week after their manager agrees.

Core hours are 11 am to 4 pm IST, when everyone must be reachable on chat.

Company laptops must use the VPN on any public Wi-Fi.

Work-from-home internet costs are reimbursed up to Rs 1,000 per month.

Team days are every Wednesday: everyone in the team comes to the office.

Working from another city for more than 2 weeks needs HR approval.
